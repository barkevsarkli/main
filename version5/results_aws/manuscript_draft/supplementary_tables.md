# Supplementary Tables

Companion to “Hybrid Activations in Multilayer Perceptrons: Learning-Rate Stability, Predictive Accuracy, and Activation Allocation.”

All values are recomputed from the original CSV files. Accuracy/F1 are percentages; pp denotes percentage points. ± is sample SD across training seeds. Primary test scores use validation-selected checkpoints. Unless specified otherwise, evaluation n=30 per cell and calibration n=6. Tables in this supplement have their own numbering.
