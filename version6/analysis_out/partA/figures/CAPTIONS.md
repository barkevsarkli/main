## mech_q1_event_study

Pure-ReLU collapse aligned at e*, the first epoch with validation accuracy below 15% (dashed line), window -8..+3 epochs. Line: median across runs; band: interquartile range. Rows: Stage 0 mlp2 MNIST (seeds 1-10, all H and LR; exploratory) and Stage 2 at the stress and below-stress LR (seeds 1-30 pooled, descriptive) for the CNN (cfg1 and cfg2, all H) and mlp1. Only late_collapse runs (outcomes.py) are shown; runs with e* = 1 have no pre-collapse window and are excluded (n per row in the label). Runs that fall below 15% and recover are counted in q1_relu_collapse (n_late_recovered, n_revived). Dead fraction: share of ReLU units (channels in conv) with z <= 0 on all 1,000 probe inputs; first layer = hidden1 (mlp2) or conv (CNN).
