## 1. "Loss > ln 10" and loss near ln 10

### 1a. Section 4.3 (Different loss patterns), line 161, and the caption at line 165

**Current (161):**
> "Mean jump sizes are about 4.57 and 4.46, and final online losses are 3.774 and 4.182
> despite final validation accuracy near 85\%. Large loss with useful accuracy is consistent
> with some very confident errors, but the two logs measure different prediction states and
> do not establish confidence calibration."

**Problem.** The loss above ln 10 comes from the CE clamp `-log(max(p_label, 1e-12))`, not from
confident errors as such. After the weight explosion the softmax is saturated, so each wrong
prediction costs exactly −ln(10⁻¹²) ≈ 27.63 and each correct one costs ≈ 0. The logged loss is
therefore 27.63 × the training error rate. In the version6 rerun these runs end at
85.7 % online training accuracy [E-S0], so the clamp accounts for the whole loss level.

**Evidence.**
- `checks/checks.txt`: clamp at `version5/main.cpp:229` and `version6/src/train_util.h:24`.
- Clamp ratio [E-S0]: all exploded leaky runs at the clamp, 50/50
  (median ratio 27.631).
- `mechanism_tables/q4_clamp_check.csv`: 931 spiked runs,
  |loss / (27.63 × error) − 1| ≤ 0.002.

**Replacement (161, from "Mean jump sizes"):**
> Mean jump sizes are about 4.57 and 4.46, and final online losses are 3.774 and 4.182 while
> final validation accuracy stays near 85\%. These losses are set by the logging clamp: once the
> softmax saturates, each misclassified training image contributes $-\ln(10^{-12})\approx27.63$
> and each correct one almost zero, so the online loss equals 27.63 times the online training
> error. In the version6 rerun of this setting, the loss divided by the training error is
> 27.63 in all 50/50 exploded leaky runs. A loss above $\ln(10)$
> therefore marks a saturated output layer, not a measure of how confident the errors are.

**Caption 165, replace** "while leaky models show a distinct large loss jump" **by** "while
leaky models show a one-epoch loss jump to a level set by the $10^{-12}$ probability clamp
(Section~\ref{sec:mechanism})".

## Outline: new section "What happens inside a collapsing network" (\label{sec:mechanism})

Replaces most of Section 6 (lines 290-301); keep line 291 (bounded vs linear path), line 299
(tanh caveat) and line 301 (width and update size).

1. **Probes.** What is recorded per epoch:
   - activation statistics on 1,000 fixed validation inputs (dead = z ≤ 0 on all of them;
     tanh saturation |z| > 3);
   - weight norms and relative updates per layer;
   - logit spread across inputs.

   Dead on the probe set is not dead on the training set (revival, paragraph 3).
   *Cites:* none (methods).
2. **ReLU collapse = last hidden layer dies; fast.**
   - Stage 0: hidden2 dead 1.00, hidden1 0.043.
   - 10 % to 90 % dead within ≤ 1 epoch in 64 % of collapses.
   - Slower at H256 (40 % ≤ 1 epoch).
   - 90 % dead reached in the epoch of e* in 536/581.

   *Cites:* figure `mech_q1_event_study`, `q1_relu_collapse.csv`, `q1_collapse_speed.csv`.
3. **Revival.**
   - 39 of 421 CIFAR-10/MNIST runs with a ≥ 99 % dead layer revive, all on CIFAR-10.
   - Revival goes with conv-weight updates (median 0.098 vs
     0.0057).

   *Cites:* `q1_revival_summary.csv`, `q1_revival_runs.csv`.
4. **Hybrid: two regimes.**
   - Protected: ReLU half alive, at LR 0.03 (0.041 dead).
   - Carried: ReLU half dead and the tanh half working, at LR 0.05 (1.00).
   - The CIFAR-10 CNN at LR 0.02 is mostly carried (150/180).

   *Cites:* figure `mech_q2_hybrid_dead`, `q2_hybrid_regimes.csv`, `q2_regime_switch.csv`.
5. **Leaky ReLU: weight explosion, not dead units.**
   - Output weight norm ×3.8e+04.
   - Saturated softmax; loss = clamp × error.
   - Spike timing fixed by (H, LR), ICC 0.989.
   - No single predictor.
   - Plateau mlp2-specific.

   *Cites:* figure `mech_q4_leaky_spike`, `q4_leaky_spikes.csv`, `q4_spike_epoch_spread.csv`,
   `q4_spike_predictors.csv`, `q4_clamp_check.csv`, `q4_spike_recovery.csv`.
6. **No reliable early warning.**
   - The loss rise precedes failure in 28 % of failing runs.
   - It is a false positive in 17 % of stable runs of the same cells.
   - AUCs 0.60-0.81.
   - Use the paper sentence in `FINDINGS_partA.md` §3.

   *Cites:* figure `mech_q6_lead_times`, `q6_auc.csv`, `q6_loss_rise_reconcile.csv`.
