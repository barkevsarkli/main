### Outcome taxonomy (used by every table from A3 on)

Each run gets one class (`analysis/outcomes.py`):

- `never_learned`: best validation accuracy < 50 % of REF, REF = the cell's mean best
  validation accuracy at the model's own best LR (cell = stage or source, dataset,
  configuration, H, model);
- `late_collapse`: learned and final validation accuracy < 15 %;
- `late_degradation`: learned, final ≥ 15 % and peak-to-final drop > 10 pp;
- `stable`: everything else.

Why the relative rule: one threshold works for both datasets and all architectures because it
is measured against what the same model reaches at its best LR (about 98 % on MNIST, 45-65 % on
CIFAR-10). The absolute rule (best < 50 % on MNIST, < 20 % on CIFAR-10) is kept as a
sensitivity check; it changes the class of 892 of 27956 runs
(`outcomes/rule_sensitivity.csv`).

Stage 2 class counts (all seeds): never_learned 941, late_collapse 619,
late_degradation 427, stable 10393 (`outcomes/outcome_counts.csv` per
stage, dataset, architecture, H, model, LR and seed set).

## 3. Mechanism (A3; per-epoch diagnostics, 1,000 probe inputs)

Definitions: e* = first epoch with validation accuracy < 15 %; a unit is dead when z ≤ 0 on all
1,000 probe (validation) inputs; conv units are channels.

**Q1. Pure-ReLU collapse.** The last hidden layer dies; the layer below does not. Stage 0 ReLU H128
LR 0.03 [E-S0, n = 10]: final dead fraction hidden2 1.00 (median),
hidden1 0.043; e* median 22.5. *Speed* (pure-ReLU late collapses, all
stages and LRs, n = 581; `q1_collapse_speed.csv`): the last hidden layer goes from 10 % to
90 % dead within ≤ 1 epoch in 64 % and within ≤ 3 epochs in 80 % of runs.
Per setting [D]: MNIST CNN ≤ 1 epoch in 55 % (n = 297; per-cell median
0-3 epochs), CIFAR-10 CNN in 73 % (n = 209; median 0-4,
max 38). Width slows it: ≤ 1 epoch in 75 % at H 64/128 vs 40 % at H256
(≥ 5 epochs: 6 % vs 30 %). Stage 0 mlp2 H128 [E-S0]: median 1.5
epochs (IQR 1.0-2.5, n = 12). The layer reaches 90 % dead in the epoch of
e* in 536/581 runs, before it in 26/581, after it in 19/581.

*Loss rise vs failure (Q1/Q6 reconciled; `q6_loss_rise_reconcile.csv`; loss rise = first epoch
with training loss > 2 × its running minimum; failing = late_collapse or late_degradation;
S0 + S2 seeds 1-30 + S3).* Over all 1188 failing runs the rise comes before the
failure epoch in 28 %; it also fires in 17 % of the 1259 stable runs of the
same cells and 12 % of all 10915 stable runs. MNIST CNN: before failure in
70 % (cfg1, n = 186) and 61 % (cfg2, n = 220), median lead to e*
13 / 9 epochs, but false positives in 48 % / 64 % of stable runs of the same
cells. CIFAR-10 CNN: before failure in 0 % (cfg1, n = 227) and 0.3 % (cfg2,
n = 331). Sentence for the paper:

> On MNIST the training loss starts rising a median 9-13 epochs before
> validation accuracy falls below 15 % in pure-ReLU CNN collapses, but the same rise appears in
> 48-64 % of the non-failing runs of the same cells, and on CIFAR-10 it almost never
> precedes failure; it marks high-LR training, not a specific warning. Once the units start
> to die, the last hidden layer goes from 10 % to 90 % dead within one epoch in
> 64 % of collapses.

*Revival* ([D], `q1_revival_summary.csv`). "Dead" means zero activation on the 1,000 probe
inputs, not on the training set. Of the 421 Stage 2 runs whose last hidden layer reached
≥ 99 % dead, 39 later ended < 50 % dead (all CIFAR-10); 39 of them reach > 15 %
validation accuracy again and 39 end ≥ 15 %. Another 103 CIFAR-10 runs revived
and died again; 103 CIFAR-10 and 176 MNIST runs stayed dead. Revival
coincides with conv-weight change: median relative conv-weight change per epoch while dead
0.098 in revived runs vs 0.0057 in the others; the conv dw is zero in
0 revived and 73 non-revived runs. Non-zero updates below a "dead" layer mean that
some of its units still fire on training inputs.

**Q2. Hybrid.** Stage 0 mlp2 H128 [E-S0]: the ReLU half of hidden2 is 0.041 dead (median) at
LR 0.03 and 1.00 at LR 0.05, where the tanh half (saturated fraction
1.000) carries the network. Stage 2 CNN at LR 0.02 [D]: on MNIST the hybrid is
"protected" (ReLU half < 50 % dead) in 180/180 runs; on CIFAR-10 it is "carried" (ReLU half ≥ 50 %
dead, final ≥ 15 %) in 150/180, protected in 10/180 and failed in 20/180. The
switch from protected to carried happens at LR 0.05 on MNIST cfg1 H128 and at LR
0.02 on CIFAR-10 cfg1 H128 (`q2_regime_switch.csv`).

**Q3. Mask placement (correlational).** Stage 2 cfg2 H 128/256, failing runs: the hidden layer's
ReLU units reach 50 % dead first in 106/111 CIFAR-10 ReLU failures and
115/119 @hidden failures. At CIFAR-10 H128 LR 0.02 [D], final hidden-layer ReLU dead
fraction: ReLU 1.000, @hidden 1.00, @conv 0.42; the conv layer itself stays
mostly alive (ReLU 0.054). Mixing tanh into the conv layer goes with a living hidden
layer; whether bounding the conv output is the cause is a Part B test.

**Q4. Leaky-ReLU weight explosion.** Stage 0 mlp2 H128 LR 0.03 [E-S0]: 10/10 runs spike; the
output-layer weight norm grows by a median factor 3.84e+04 in one epoch (epochs
13-16); logit spread across inputs at the spike 9.4e+10; max softmax
probability 1.00; at most 0.016 of units dead. The jump grows with LR
(×6e+03 at 0.02, ×5.3e+05 at 0.05) and the spike comes earlier (epochs
28-30 vs 3-5). Within an (H, LR) cell the spike epoch hardly varies: ICC
0.989 (S0 mlp2), 0.999 / 0.999 (MNIST cfg1 / cfg2), 0.94 /
0.93 (CIFAR-10). No single pre-spike quantity is constant across cells (S0 coefficient of
variation: hidden1 weight norm 0.33, LR × output w² 6.2). After the spike the run
sits at the plateau: S0 LR 0.03 validation 92.9 % before, 76.8 % at the spike,
86.0 % final. The plateau is mlp2-specific: MNIST cfg1 LR 0.02 recovers to 96.5 %
and CIFAR-10 cfg1 LR 0.02 ends at 25.7 % [D]. The training loss after the spike equals
the clamp value times the training error rate: over 931 spiked runs and 30967
post-spike epochs, |loss / (27.63 × error) − 1| ≤ 0.002.

**Q5. Partial collapse.** late_degradation runs ending below 50 %: Stage 0 18, Stage 2
357, Stage 3 6 (`q5_partial_collapse.csv`: predicted classes and surviving units per
layer).

**Q6. Early warning.** ROC AUC of a signal at epoch e for "failure in e+1..e+3" (negatives:
epochs of stable runs of the same cell): loss rise 0.49 / 0.60 / 0.62
(S0 / S2 / S3), max |z| jump 0.65 / 0.63 / 0.53, dead fraction > 20 %
0.65 / 0.52 / 0.30, dw spike 0.81 / 0.56 / 0.54.
The best S0 signal (dw spike) fires ≥ 1 epoch before failure in 0 of 66 failing
runs. No signal warns reliably.

**Depth.** Late collapse after learning in pure ReLU needs two ReLU layers (mlp2, or conv +
hidden). mlp1 ReLU never collapses late: MNIST 0/390, CIFAR-10
0/390 Stage 2 runs; CIFAR-10 mlp1 ReLU instead never learns
(150/390 runs; every one of its final < 15 % runs at H128 is never_learned, share
1.00). Matched at LR 0.02 [D]: CIFAR-10 CNN ReLU late_collapse 175/180, mlp1
late_collapse 0/90 and never_learned 90/90. Across all mlp1 runs (Stages 1-2),
6 meet the late_collapse rule, none of them pure ReLU (leaky_relu 5, tanh+relu 1; cifar10;
best ≤ 32.2 %, final 12.0-14.6 %): near-chance degradations of weakly
trained runs, not the dead-layer collapse. The data support: *late collapse of pure ReLU
networks occurs with two ReLU layers on both datasets and never with one; with one layer,
high LR either degrades (MNIST) or prevents learning (CIFAR-10).*

## 7. Discrepancies with §1.4 / §1.5 of the Part A prompt

   LR (×6e+03 to ×5.3e+05). The 85 % plateau is mlp2-specific; the CNN
   recovers on MNIST and ends near 26 % on CIFAR-10.
4. **§1.5-4 (hybrid regimes).** Confirmed (0.041 dead at LR 0.03,
   1.00 at 0.05).
5. **§1.5-5 (mask placement).** Confirmed, with one addition: at CIFAR-10 H256 conv-only mixing

6. **§1.5-6 (mlp1).** Not as stated. CIFAR-10 mlp1 ReLU does not collapse late: its failures are

7. **§1.5-7 (tuned accuracy).** MNIST -0.11 to +0.03 pp (one cell slightly beyond ±0.1);
   CIFAR-10 CNN H 64/128 -0.91 to -0.46 pp, so the lower end is below the stated 0.5 pp

8. **A8 premises checked against the data.** "Hybrid beats tanh at H ≥ 128 at the stress LR"

9. **New: ReLU failure is non-monotone in LR** in version5 and Stage 0 mlp2 H128

## 8. Claims the data do not support

- A general tuned-accuracy gain from mixing (T5: none on MNIST, a cost on CIFAR-10 CNN at H 64/128).
- "Slow death" of ReLU units: the loss rises early, the units die fast.
- The training-loss rise as an early warning (false positives 17 % in the same cells;
  AUC 0.60 in Stage 2).
- "Very confident errors" behind leaky loss > ln 10: it is the 1e-12 clamp times the error rate.
- A single weight-norm threshold that predicts the leaky spike.
- Hybrid ≥ tanh everywhere: MNIST CNN H 64/128 tanh is higher at the stress LR.
- ρ = 0.5 as the best share in general.
- That bounding the conv output *causes* the CIFAR-10 protection (Q3 is correlational).
- A meaningful K choice in Stage 1 cfg1 (margins 0.02 pp) or a robust CIFAR-10 cfg2 choice.
- Mixing as the most stable option at high LR: sigmoid is more stable in 17/18 cells.
